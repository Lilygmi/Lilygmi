Projects

  • Characterizing the carbon fingerprints of deep convection events and open-ocean polynyas in the Southern Ocean across CMIP6 models, isolating their signature on air–sea carbon exchange.
  • Identifying interannual biogeochemical variability across the Southern Ocean — phytoplankton phenology, nutrient co-limitation — from satellite composites and ARGO float data, and assessing likely responses to climate change.
  • Utilized PyCO2SYS software in Python to understand and run the ocean carbon chemistry subroutines used in climate models and evaluate carbonate chemistry across the Southern Ocean.
  • Investigated ecological drivers of phytoplankton productivity in the iron- and light-limited North Pacific and North Atlantic subpolar regions, evaluating CMIP6 models against observational products (World Ocean Atlas, satellite chlorophyll-a and phytoplankton biomass products); co-author on manuscript in preparation.
